📜 Peg Solitaire Rules 📜

🎯 Objective

Your goal is to leave only one peg on the board, preferably in the center position.

🎮 How to Play

  • The game starts with pegs on the board, with one empty hole in the center.
  • A peg may jump over an adjacent peg (horizontally, vertically, obliquely) into an empty space directly beyond it.
  • The peg that was jumped over is removed from the board.
  • Jumps can be made only in four directions: up, down, left, or right.
  • Diagonal jumps are not allowed.

❌ Game Over

  • The game ends when no more valid moves are possible.
  • If only one peg remains on the board — you win!
  • The fewer pegs left, the better your result.
