Peg Solitaire Rules

🎯 The Goal

Your goal is to leave only one peg on the board, preferably in the center position.

🎮 How to Play

The game starts with pegs on the board, with one empty hole in the center.

A peg can jump over an adjacent peg (horizontally, vertically or diagonally) to the empty space behind it.

The jumped peg is removed from the board.

❌ End of the Game

The game ends when you can't make any more moves, you lose.

If there is only one peg left, you win!

The fewer pegs left, the better your score.
